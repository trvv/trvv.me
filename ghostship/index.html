Preface

Let's see if we can run a company with just an AI. Specifically, I want to see if the principles and practices used in real-world organizational structures are functional over "agents." A design note: the greatest thing for an "agent" to have is a clear win condition, a boolean one if you can help it. This is why they're great at things like CTFs; you either get the flag or you don't. There are no partial solutions or half steps, it's just victory or defeat with unlimited attempts. This is antithetical to people in general, but is more applicable to "employees" (you don't get fired, or you do).
